## Active July 2011 MEDEVAC unit

The April 2011 `Eye of the Falcon` (`pdf_8632.pdf`) records a transfer of MEDEVAC responsibility on 25 March 2011:

```text
incoming:
C Company, 5th Battalion, 159th Aviation Regiment
Cowboy Dustoff
Wyoming Army National Guard

outgoing:
C Company, 2nd Battalion, 104th Aviation Regiment
West Virginia Army National Guard
```

Cowboy Dustoff provided helicopter medical evacuation support to Task Force Tigershark in Khost Province and surrounding eastern Afghanistan.

## Mixed Task Force Tigershark aircraft presence

The January 2011 `Eye of the Falcon` (`pdf_8055.pdf`) directly documents in the Khost/Task Force Tigershark context:

- CH-47 sling-load operations with fuel blivets;
- OH-58 maintenance;
- UH-60 transport activity.

AH-64 presence is independently established by the July 2011 ORBAT and contemporary brigade reporting.

These records confirm type presence. They do not by themselves establish the full local inventory.

## CH-47 scope remains narrow

The binding OMW value of six CH-47 at Salerno applies only to the separately documented B/7-158 deployment-pool reconstruction.

```text
6 CH-47 from B/7-158 pool
!= all CH-47 that may have operated from Salerno
```

Mission presence, temporary attachment, SOTF support and other company contributions remain separate evidence categories.

## Manifest consequences

The Salerno manifest must identify at least these separate rotary-wing pools:

```text
AH-64 attack component — TF Tigershark
OH-58 reconnaissance component — TF Tigershark
UH-60 assault/utility component — TF Tigershark
UH-60 MEDEVAC component — C/5-159 Cowboy Dustoff
CH-47 heavy-lift component — explicitly scoped unit pool(s)
```

Army MEDEVAC must not be merged with utility UH-60 inventory or with USAF rescue assets.

## Strength boundary

The sources resolve the active MEDEVAC company but do not state its exact aircraft count at Salerno. Any local OMW count remains a project reconstruction and must retain that label.
