## Mountain Dustoff mission presence

The February and April 2011 `Eye of the Falcon` publications document C Company, 3-10 Aviation Regiment, Mountain Dustoff, flying UH-60 MEDEVAC missions in Nangarhar/Kunar and using Jalalabad Airfield for patient transfer and onward treatment.

Binding interpretation:

```text
MEDEVAC mission or patient-transfer presence at Jalalabad
!= permanent local Mountain Dustoff inventory
```

These aircraft remain accounted to their assigned MEDEVAC organization and forward-location model unless a source explicitly assigns them to the Fenty inventory.

## CH-47 attachment boundary

Contemporary 10th CAB reporting establishes that B Company, 3-10 Aviation was reassigned operationally from TF Phoenix to the Fenty task force during the rotation.

Therefore:

```text
organic parent unit
!= operational task-force assignment
!= permanent peacetime home station
```

The active Fenty CH-47 inventory must identify which unit pool is being counted. It must not silently combine B/3-10, B/7-158 or other attached CH-47 elements.
