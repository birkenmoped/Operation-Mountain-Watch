## AH71: Spring 2009, No. 71

### Integrated article

- Dirk D. Ringgenberg, “The Battle of Baluch Kalay.”
- Event date: 3 May 2005.
- Area: Arghandab Valley, Zabul Province.
- Friendly unit: Company C, 2d Battalion (Airborne), 503d Infantry, 173d Airborne Brigade.
- Source character: participant-authored tactical narrative.

### Key extracted evidence

- scout platoon and small ANP contingent engaged by Taliban;
- one Humvee destroyed by RPG;
- FOB Lagman outside Qalat as the launching base;
- approximately 50-mile distance and 45-minute helicopter transit;
- 2 x CH-47 in first lift with approximately 60 personnel;
- later lift with approximately 28 personnel;
- 2 x AH-64 initial escort/security;
- later A-10 support;
- one platoon retained for FOB security;
- troops prepared for up to 72 hours;
- CH-47 struck by RPG near the tail during approach;
- forced use of an unplanned landing zone and split company disposition;
- Arghandab River approximately 50–75 m wide;
- dense orchard terrain, high compound walls and a critical improvised bridge;
- enemy use of high ground, RPGs, unsecured radios, bridge-denial intent and civilian disguise;
- limitations on AH-64 employment due to vegetation, uncertain targets and friendly proximity;
- 60-mm mortar element not located at the point of need;
- interpreter contributed signals exploitation, local knowledge and tactical judgment.

### Integrated destination

- `docs/79-baloch-kalay-air-assault-and-company-combat-2005.md`

### Source cautions

- participant perspective;
- enemy strength and casualty claims are source-reported;
- 2005 task organization does not define 2010–2011 OMW ORBAT;
- article terminology is retained where uncertain rather than silently corrected.
