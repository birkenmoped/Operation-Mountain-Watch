# CH-47 unit-pool scope addendum — 2011

## Source hierarchy

Primary July 2011 unit/location authority:

```text
122362406-Afghanistan-order-of-battle-july-2011.pdf
```

Contemporary supplemental sources include the 2011 `Eye of the Falcon` publications issued by the 10th Combat Aviation Brigade.

## Parallel CH-47 contributors

The contemporary brigade record demonstrates that multiple CH-47 companies or detachments could contribute to one task force or base during the same rotation.

Documented examples include:

```text
B Company, 3-10 Aviation
operationally reassigned from TF Phoenix to the Fenty task force

B Company, 1-168 Aviation
attached to TF Phoenix at Bagram

B Company, 7-158 Aviation
contributed to TF Knighthawk operations

B Company, 2-135 Aviation
also contributed to TF Knighthawk operations
```

## Binding scope rule

The OMW reconstruction:

```text
Bagram: 13
FOB Salerno: 6
FOB Shank: 6
Total: 25
```

applies exclusively to the specifically documented B/7-158 deployment pool.

It does not represent:

- every CH-47 assigned to Task Force Falcon/10th CAB;
- every CH-47 assigned to TF Phoenix;
- every CH-47 operating from Bagram, Salerno or Shank;
- all CH-47 in Afghanistan;
- the total U.S. Army CH-47 inventory.

## Shank-specific consequence

The August 2011 brigade publication identifies personnel from both B/7-158 and B/2-135 in TF Knighthawk CH-47 operations.

Therefore:

```text
6 B/7-158 CH-47 reconstructed at Shank
!= complete local Shank CH-47 inventory across all units
```

## Fenty-specific consequence

The reassignment of B/3-10 to the Fenty task force proves that operational basing and organic parentage can differ during the rotation.

No CH-47 count may be transferred between base manifests solely from the parent battalion name.

## Bagram-specific consequence

B/1-168 attachment to TF Phoenix is a separate unit contribution. It must not be absorbed into the B/7-158 13-aircraft Bagram share without explicit evidence.

## Required documentation tuple

Every CH-47 record must identify:

```text
company
parent battalion/regiment
task-force attachment
location
date or period
assigned count, visible count or mission-presence status
source
```

A base-level count without unit scope is non-compliant with this decision.
